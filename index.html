QUICK GUIDE
Arrange, align, inspect.
Focus. ⛶ Focus or Ctrl+Shift+F fills the screen with the two channels, keeping cycle time, each spindle's waiting and a timeline in view. Esc or the button exits.
Move code. Drag a block by its dotted handle within its channel. The arrows or Alt+↑ / Alt+↓ move the selected block. A toolpath includes everything up to its ending N line, including waits and B indexes inside it; those pieces show a chip like N40 · 2/5. Drag ⧉ or press Alt+Shift+↑ / ↓ to move the whole toolpath. Drag a wait or macro block onto a line inside any code block to insert it right there; a green line marks the spot. Moving a wait across an N line moves it into the neighbouring toolpath.
Edit in place. Click any line. Edits are regrouped and aligned when you leave the block. Source view gives you both full programs.
Read the links. Green links are paired barriers. Purple links are a shared-macro signal and wait. Alignment keeps source order; conflicting waits appear in review notes.
Estimate the cycle. Enter feed rates and tool-change seconds in Settings. Select a block to override its duration. The timeline schedules both channels independently and adds waiting when a dependency holds a channel.
Save. The current workspace is autosaved in this browser. Export a workspace JSON to back it up and restore it elsewhere. NC downloads never include visual spacers.
Preview limits. Only the selected pair is modeled. Subprograms, branches, cutting contact, transforms and machine-specific behavior require your existing simulator. A complete timing estimate is not a machine safety check.
